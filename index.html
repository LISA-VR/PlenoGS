Abstract

We present PlenoGS, a method that adapts the 3DGS framework to train directly on raw plenoptic images. We derive a differentiable plenoptic projection model that maps 3D Gaussians through both the main lens and the micro-lens array, and introduce a microlens-aware rasterization stage.
